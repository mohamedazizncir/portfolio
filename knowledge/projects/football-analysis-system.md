## Football player analysis and stats tracking system

Personal project (02/2026), grown out of football as a hobby rather than a career target.

**Technical approach:** YOLOv8 fine-tuned for multi-object detection and tracking of players, K-Means clustering to classify players by team color, optical flow combined with perspective transformation to compute real player speed and distance from video.

**Status:** personal, currently private.
