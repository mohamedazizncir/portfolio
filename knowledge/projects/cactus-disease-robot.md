## Autonomous Cactus Disease Detection Robot

Autonomous robot for detecting disease in prickly pear cactus plants. Computer vision with MobileNet, navigation handled with Arduino.

Placed 2nd at the IEEE Tunisian Engineering Day.

Built between 10/2024 and 11/2024, using YOLOv8 and OpenCV in an earlier version of the vision pipeline.
