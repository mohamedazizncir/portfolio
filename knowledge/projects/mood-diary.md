## Mood Diary

Flutter mobile app with sentiment analysis on diary entries, using a DistilBERT model served through a Flask backend. Integrated with Firebase Auth and Firestore for accounts and storage.

Along the way, debugged an ADB deployment issue specific to a Xiaomi/MIUI device.
