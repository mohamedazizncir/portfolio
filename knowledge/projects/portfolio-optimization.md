## Portfolio optimization (PFA2)

Academic project. Implemented mean-variance portfolio optimization models with chance-constrained and kurtosis-based formulations. Built a Sequential Quadratic Programming (SQP) solver and benchmarked it against semidefinite programming (SDP) relaxations.

Aziz considers this his first academic introduction to the world of finance.
