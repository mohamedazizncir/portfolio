## STEG IoT monitoring platform (STEGPoint)

Built during the STEG internship (Sousse, 06/2025 to 08/2025). Real-time IoT data collection and equipment monitoring system for STEG, plus an HR platform called STEGPoint.

Included a WhatsApp chatbot that gave workers remote access to sensor readings and sent notifications during emergencies.

**Stack:** Raspberry Pi, MQTT, Node-RED, Hugging Face, Llama.

**Scale:** more than 100 sensors across 11 units, used by 5 maintenance directors and engineers.
