## CRMN internship, Sousse (06/2026 to 08/2026)

Centre de Recherche en Microélectronique et Nanotechnologie. Official title: "Contextual Agentic Reasoning via RAG and Interoperability APIs in Home-Monitoring Frameworks." Worked on a real-time home health-monitoring system with a two-tier architecture connected only through a Kafka message broker (Tier 1: fast, rule-based; Tier 2: slower, AI reasoning-based).

Aziz worked on contextual agentic reasoning for Tier 2 (choosing the orchestration framework, then extending the pipeline to reason over enriched context across multiple scenarios), and on the RAG retrieval stack and interoperability services connecting the tiers. Benchmarked four vector databases and several orchestration frameworks for latency, reliability, and scalability before integrating the best combination. Stack: RAG, ChromaDB, FAISS, Qdrant, Llama, Kafka, Docker, LangGraph, CrewAI, RAGAS, Git, Python.

The internship is finished. He is now continuing this work with his supervisor toward a research publication, and it gave him a genuine interest in agentic AI research.
