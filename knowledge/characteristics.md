## Coming back from injury

Tore my ACL and went through surgery. The recovery period meant weeks of not being able to do the thing I loved most, which taught me a level of patience I didn't have before, showing up for slow, unglamorous physical therapy work with no immediate payoff. Getting back to playing sports afterward was less about physical recovery and more about not giving up on something after a real setback. It's the same mindset I bring to a hard technical problem: staying with it past the point where quitting would be easier.
